Good To Know

Pricing questions, answered.

Why isn't pricing published on the site?

Every school's student count, campus setup, and feature needs are different, so we quote per school rather than publish a one-size-fits-all rate.

Can we start on Starter and move to Growth later?

Yes. Your data carries over — nothing is re-entered when you upgrade.

Is there a setup or onboarding fee?

This is covered in your quote up front — no surprise charges once you're live.

How do we get an exact quote?

Book a free demo and tell us your student count and campus setup — we'll follow up with a quote for your school.
