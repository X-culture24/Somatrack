Fair By Design

Built to work for every school, not just the ones with an IT budget.

Common questions from schools deciding whether to make the switch.

Do we need to buy new computers?

No. Somatrack runs in a browser on the computers and phones you already have. The only optional extra is a fingerprint scanner at the gate for biometric attendance.

What about schools with unreliable internet?

Parents get SMS on any phone, no data bundle needed. In NOVA LMS, quiz answers stay saved on the device if the connection drops and are sent the moment it's back.

Does payroll handle PAYE, NSSF, SHIF and the housing levy?

Yes. Deductions are calculated from rates your finance office can edit, alongside allowances, salary advances and loans — then salaries are paid straight to M-Pesa.

Which payment channels does it support?

M-Pesa paybill and STK push through Safaricom, Equity (Jenga) or KCB, plus cash, cheque and bank payments recorded at the bursar. Every payment posts to the right learner and texts the family a receipt.

Is there a long-term contract?

No lock-in. You can stop using Somatrack at the end of any billing period.

What if our staff aren't comfortable with technology?

If you can use WhatsApp, you can use Somatrack — most staff are confident within a day.

Can every teacher and parent use it, or just the office?

Everyone. The office, bursar, teachers, parents and students each get their own view, on the web or in the mobile app.
