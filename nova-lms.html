Built For Exam Day

Quizzes and CATs you can trust.

NOVA keeps time on the server, not the student's browser, so every attempt is fair and nothing gets lost.

Timed by the server

The clock starts when the student opens the quiz and can't be paused or reset. When time is up, the attempt submits itself.

Safe on a bad connection

If the internet drops, answers stay on the device and are sent the moment it's back.

Typed answers only

Pasting, dropping and bulk inserts are blocked in answer boxes, so written answers are the student's own.

Attempt limits

Set how many attempts each quiz allows. Students see unanswered and flagged questions before they submit.

Fair deadlines

Assignment submissions are timestamped by the server. Late work is either accepted and marked late, or blocked — your choice.

Tied to fees

Grades and exam cards are released once a student's fee balance is cleared, with no extra work for the office.
